Our story

It started in Dang's backyard.

Just a pot, a propane burner, and Viet Cajun garlic butter — the way his family makes it. Dang cooked for his wife, his kids, and the friends who'd swing by on weekends. Word got out.

What began as a Saturday tradition for family and the local Vietnamese community quickly became something bigger. Friends brought friends. Posts went up on Facebook. Strangers started messaging asking for a 5 lb bag, then 10, then 50. Backyard Boil grew because of word-of-mouth — and because Dang refused to lose what made it special: knowing every customer by name, checking in on how their week's going, treating every order like he's still cooking for family.

Today Dang runs through up to 2,000 pounds of crawfish a week during peak season — hand-boiling every batch, managing every order, leading every line, and somehow still finding time to text customers back personally.

“ Cook with care. Treat people right. Feed them like family. ”

That's it. That's Backyard Boil.
